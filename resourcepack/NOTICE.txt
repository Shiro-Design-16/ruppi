Furigana resource pack - Minecraft 26.3 build
=============================================

Contents
  * Furigana v4 (+1) base keys - unchanged, rubi native notation.
  * Every key the base pack has no entry for was written out for this build
    in the rubi notation ("§^base(reading)", okurigana outside the brackets).
    "Alex's Japanese Furigana Language" (ja_fw.json) was consulted as a
    reference for the readings; this pack ships only our own generated
    language file. Its ja_fe variant (readings collected at the end of the
    sentence) cannot be mapped back onto individual words and was not used.
